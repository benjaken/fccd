# FCCD customer portal

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users and purpose
Personal catering customers use a verified email account to view orders, delivery arrangements and receipts, add items to an existing order and manage contact details. Organization members have a separate authorized workspace.

## Confirmed personal workflow
The personal home prioritizes the next delivery and repeat ordering. Brand menu entries and new purchases open the corresponding Shopify store. Repeat ordering should preselect original products and quantities when they can be mapped safely. Existing-order add-ons remain separate. Address management should eventually share Shopify's address book. Payment evidence requires financial review and an internal email notification.

## Constraints
Use email OTP; no customer passwords and no additional phone-matching step. Phone is contact information only. Preserve the existing FCCD brand, shared header, mobile drawer, Traditional Chinese and English translations. Never show organization balances as personal rewards. Unconnected Shopify identity/address synchronization and personal reward rules must be represented honestly.

## Open integrations
Shopify customer authorization, multiple-store address strategy, configurable-package repeat ordering and individual reward issuance/redemption rules are not yet implemented or confirmed.
