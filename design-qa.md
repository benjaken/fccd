# Design QA summary

- The order-status picker stays compact and its option list floats above surrounding content.
- Delivery notes use the established right-side panel and retain printable A4 content.
- Customer messages keep order context, category counts, history, and the note composer.

## Restaurant operations

- Daily sales supports responsive entry, restored legacy values, cash totals, and clear loading states.
- Daily purchases supports supplier-first entry and narrow layouts.

## Receipt and invoice PDFs

- REC and INV editors support clause selection, delivery fees, grouped pagination, and page controls.
- Addresses wrap correctly, delivery times remain visible, prices stay centered, and document titles follow the approved alignment.
